## 中文

一次散步、读到的一句话，或一个还没想明白的问题，都可以成为一篇随笔的开始。

不必急着得出结论。先记下来，过一段时间再读，也许会看见新的角度。

## English

A walk, a sentence from a book, or an unanswered question can be the beginning of an essay.

There is no need to reach a conclusion immediately. Write the thought down and revisit it later; a new perspective may emerge.
