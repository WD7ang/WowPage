Every project begins with a question. This sample note demonstrates a simple way to share a small experiment and its outcome.

## The question

What changes when we simplify the problem before choosing a method?

## The experiment

Start with a small baseline, change one variable, and record the result. Keeping the setup clear makes the next experiment easier to understand.

## What comes next

Write down both the useful result and the question that remains. Replace this example with your own research notes.
